# Brand Foundations — Conductia

## Name

**Conductia.** Single word, no hyphen, no space. The canonical spelling is
"Conductia" in all contexts — product, marketing, legal, code identifiers.
The transcript variant "Conductia" is transcription noise and is not used.

Source: decision-log D4 (Lance, explicit, 2026-05-19)

---

## Positioning

**"The compliance-first M&A intelligence layer for managing directors."**

This line is precise and non-negotiable. Each word carries load:

- **Compliance-first** — compliance is the architecture, not a feature. It runs
  before, during, and after every user action.
- **M&A intelligence layer** — the platform sits above raw data and below the
  MD's decision. It does not replace the MD; it removes the work that shouldn't
  reach them.
- **Managing directors** — not analysts. The audience is senior, sell-side,
  under regulatory scrutiny, and time-constrained. Every UX and copy decision
  is calibrated for this person.

Source: design spec §11; decision-log D4

## Voice

**Precise, senior, decisive. Anti-data-dump.**

The voice the product and all communications use:

| Principle | What it means |
|---|---|
| Precise | Every word earns its place. No hedging, no qualifiers, no filler. |
| Senior | The register is peer-to-peer with an MD, not instructional. |
| Decisive | Statements, not questions. Verdicts, not options. |
| Anti-data-dump | Surface the decision, not the dataset. One clear answer beats ten possibilities. |

**Do not:**
- Explain what the product does in copy the user has already seen.
- Use softening language ("perhaps", "might", "could potentially").
- Front-load context before getting to the point.
- Use scare-quotes or meta-narration ("the so-called compliance layer").

Source: design spec §2, §11

## Brand promise (internal anchor)

Conductia gives an MD the intelligence they need to make a sell-side decision
— with every action already compliant. The platform removes the distance between
intent and confidence.

This is not a tagline. It is the test every product and brand decision is held
against.

Source: design spec §1, §2; `00-vision/strategy-and-moat.md`
