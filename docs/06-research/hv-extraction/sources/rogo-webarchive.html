May 2026

Slide Annotations

Drop a comment on any slide and Felix revises in place. No rewriting prompts. No starting over.


How it works

Comment in context

Open any PPTX-backed slide deck attached to a chat and annotate the slides directly — no copy-pasting, no describing what you mean in prose.

Felix revises in place

Each annotation becomes a targeted instruction. Felix applies the change to the slide and keeps the rest of the deck intact.

Iterate without starting over

Keep adding annotations as the deck evolves. Your earlier work stays put.




May 2026

Memory

Memory lets Felix carry your preferences across every conversation: your role, formatting rules, recurring conventions, and the companies you work with most.


What Felix remembers

Your role and context

Tell Felix once whether you cover consumer and retail, infrastructure, or any other coverage area — and it will frame future answers accordingly.

Formatting conventions

Prefer tables over prose? Comps in millions instead of billions? Felix picks up on these preferences and applies them across chats.

Recurring companies and themes

The teams, tickers, and themes you ask about most often stay top of mind so Felix can anticipate context.


Managing your memory

Open Settings → Memory to view everything Felix has stored, edit individual entries, or clear memories you no longer need.

January 2026

Introducing Projects

Running a sell-side process? You need instant access to your financial model, call notes, marketing materials, and legal documents to execute analyses throughout the deal lifecycle.

Projects gives you a complete workspace where your Rogo chats stay organized, files are always accessible, and context persists across every conversation.


What Makes Projects So Useful?

1. Organize Chats Into Folders

Keep all related chats in a single Project folder—whether you're working on a deal, sector analysis, or ongoing client coverage. Everything stays organized and searchable.

2. Persistent Instructions

Set instructions that apply to every conversation in your Project—no need to repeat yourself. Configure Rogo with the right context to support your work, ensuring every response is tailored to your specific workstream.

3. Complete File Management

Build a Project Library that Rogo can draw on for every response. Bulk upload your dataroom files and continuously add materials as the deal progresses.

Reference specific documents directly within a conversation to ground responses with precision—whether it’s an expert transcript, a credit agreement, or a model—rather than querying your entire folder.

Easily view your files in-browser at any time within your workspace for quick navigation and reference.


The Bottom Line

Projects transforms Rogo from a conversation tool into a complete workspace for complex financial work. Everything you need—organized, persistent, and exactly where you expect it.




December 2025

Sheets Agent

A few months ago, Rogo launched a Sheets Agent optimized for single-tab workflows. It executed simple DCFs, P&L analyses, and other financial tasks with complete reliability.

Since then, we've been working to expand its functionality and make it unlike any other sheets agent on the market. This agent can now handle complex multi-tab models and analyses without sacrificing accuracy. Today, we're excited to say it's available in Rogo.


What Makes Our Sheets Agent Different?

1. Automatic Spreadsheet Creation

The user doesn't have to go to a separate tab or instruct Rogo to create a spreadsheet. We've taught our model to know when the user wants a spreadsheet, so it will open one up automatically and start editing when it makes sense.

2. High-Fidelity Financial Data Sources

Our agent uses high-fidelity financial data sources to build models and analyses, as opposed to relying on the model's knowledge alone.

3. In-Cell Citations

Every hardcode is cited in-cell back to the exact source, so you can trust and audit all outputs fast.

4. Built Like a Banker Would

Our agent builds sheets like a real banker would, with clean structure and proper conventions.


The Bottom Line

This took extensive steering and close collaboration between our engineering and banking teams.

When you use Rogo, you'll have access to a sheets agent that will produce deliverables you'll actually use.

Expanded Access to S&P Capital IQ Datasets

You can now gain access to new S&P Global datasets — expanding the high-value data available directly in Rogo.

S&P Capital IQ Ownership

The first dataset available is Capital IQ Ownership. The S&P Capital IQ Ownership dataset provides detailed institutional, mutual fund and insiders/individuals equity share ownership data for public companies along with public float shares, buys and sells of insiders and major shareholders. In addition, you can access deep insights into portfolio data on investments for Institutions and Funds. This dataset can be used to track market momentum and identify the frequency by which assets within a fund are bought and sold.

This dataset is available to S&P Global clients with the appropriate agreement in place and marks the first of several S&P Global Capital IQ datasets being integrated into Rogo including S&P Capital IQ Transactions and S&P Capital IQ Company Intelligence. To learn more about Capital IQ Ownership, inclusive of exploring sample data and the data dictionary, visit the S&P Global Marketplace here.

August 2025

Rogo Pro

What is Rogo Pro?

  • Rogo Pro uses our proprietary reasoning model specifically trained for financial Q&A.
  • It's built to handle complex, multi-step queries that require deeper analysis.

When to Use Rogo Pro?

  • For a quick summary of Apple’s latest earnings transcript, base Rogo is perfect and faster.
  • For developing a detailed bull and bear case on Apple, incorporating their latest earnings transcript and other sources, Rogo Pro is the right choice. It can reason through the many components of a strong bull and bear case, providing a more intelligent answer.

Tips and Data Sources

  • When in doubt, try your query in both base Rogo and Rogo Pro.
  • Rogo Pro currently has access to all of the same sources as base Rogo, plus some additional ones like non-US filings. More data sources will be added over time.
