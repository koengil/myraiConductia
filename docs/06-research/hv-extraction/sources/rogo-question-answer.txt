Q&A: 

	1	Once we’ve uploaded files to the data room - can we search across deal files / have the files been tagged to relate to a certain deal? will the agents know to search those specific files only? is there an automatic tagging or a way to tag files so we can split them based on the deal they are related to? do you provide connectors for our existing filesystems (ie. google drive, sharepoint)? 
	2	How can we share documents to other parties that need to them? (ie. signing an NDA, or sharing a pitch deck). does this happen through the platform or do we need to download and send separately?


=========

Great set of questions — these all come down to how Rogo handles your deal files, so let me walk through each one.

1. Organizing deal files: use Projects
The right home for data-room files is a Project. A Project is a persistent workspace where you upload all your deal documents (financial model, call notes, marketing materials, legal docs, VDR exports) and they stay available across every chat in that Project.
	•	Create one per deal: Click New Project from the sidebar, name it after the deal, then Add Files (drag-and-drop or browse — PDF, PowerPoint, Word, Excel, and images; plus .txt, .md, .json, .html, .xml, .tsv, .csv when uploading via Felix). You can upload individual files or whole folders, up to ~100 files per project.
	•	This is effectively your “tagging by deal.” Rather than tagging individual files, you separate deals by putting each deal’s files in its own Project. That’s the cleaner mental model — one Project = one deal’s document universe.
2. Will the agents search only that deal’s files?
Yes — that’s exactly what Projects give you, and you have two levels of control:
	•	Project scoping: When you work inside a Project, Rogo draws on that Project’s library — not files from other deals/Projects.
	•	File context toggles: Within a Project you can toggle specific files as “context” for your next chat, so you can point Rogo at, say, just the credit agreement or just the model for a given question. You can also reference a specific document directly in a conversation to ground the answer precisely.
There isn’t a separate free-form “tag” field on individual files, and there’s no automatic per-file deal tagging — the deal separation is achieved through the Project structure itself. If you want everything in one Project but need finer slicing, the file-context toggles are the tool for that.
One thing worth flagging: Projects do not currently pull context from your previous chats in the same Project — they share the files and Project instructions, not prior conversation history. So the “memory” of a Project is its document library plus the persistent instructions you set, not past threads.
3. Connectors for Google Drive / SharePoint
Yes — Rogo has read-only connectors for both, plus several others:
	•	SharePoint — searches files, folders, and OneNote across your SharePoint sites; available in all Rogo modes + Felix. Comes in Full Access or a Limited variant (restricted to admin-selected sites).
	•	Google Drive — Docs, Sheets, Slides, PDFs, and other Drive files; Felix only.
	•	OneDrive, Box, Egnyte are also available (Felix only), and there are Microsoft 365 / Google productivity connectors (Outlook, Teams, Gmail, Calendar, Notion) plus Salesforce.
A few important notes:
	•	All connectors are read-only — Rogo can search and read, never modify, move, or delete anything.
	•	They inherit your existing permissions (e.g., SharePoint access flows through Microsoft Graph, so you only see what you already have rights to). Rogo doesn’t index/store the files — it retrieves at query time.
	•	Availability is admin-gated. Your org’s administrator enables each connector, and then you authorize your own account under Account Settings → Connections. If you don’t see Google Drive or SharePoint there, it likely hasn’t been turned on for your organization yet — your account team can sort that out.
4. Sharing documents to other parties
Here’s the important distinction:
Inside Rogo — internal collaboration only. Project Sharing lets you give colleagues access to a Project (Owner / Editor / Viewer roles) via the Share button in the project title bar. But this is scoped to users, teams, or your organization within your own Rogo tenant — it’s not a way to grant access to an outside counterparty. Also note: when you share a thread or export internally, colleagues can’t download the underlying files off a shared thread — you have to send the actual file yourself.
To external parties (counterparty who signed an NDA, sending a pitch deck to a buyer) — you download and send separately. Rogo isn’t a virtual data room or an external file-distribution/e-signature platform, and there’s no documented workflow for sharing files out to non-Rogo third parties or for executing NDAs through the platform. The pattern is: build/pull the materials in Rogo, export them (Word, PowerPoint, PDF, or Excel — with a ZIP option to bundle a PDF with its source files), then distribute through your usual channel (email, your VDR provider, DocuSign for the NDA, etc.).
So think of Rogo as the place to organize, search, and produce your deal materials — and your existing tools as the place to distribute and sign with outside parties.
