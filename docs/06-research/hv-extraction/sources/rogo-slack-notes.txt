Jess Mah  [12:04 PM]
Here’s what I can tell you from Rogo’s documented practices, organized by your four questions. Where the documentation stops short of a firm, diligence-grade answer, I’ll flag it rather than guess.
Training — the clearest point
Rogo does not train its models on your content. Per Rogo’s documentation, your prompts, files, and outputs are never used to train any Rogo or third-party model. This is stated as a core policy, and it’s meant to let you use real deal materials, live client situations, and proprietary data.
How query data is handled
	•	Connectors are read-only and use OAuth. For integrations (SharePoint, Salesforce, FactSet, Google, etc.), you authenticate through the provider’s own login — your credentials are never stored by Rogo, and Rogo can only access what your account is already permissioned to see. Nothing is created, edited, deleted, or sent through these connectors.
	•	SharePoint/OneDrive is retrieved at query time, not indexed. Rogo queries Microsoft’s Graph API on demand and does not index or store those files — data is pulled for the session and not retained beyond it.
	•	Excel plugin minimizes what’s transmitted. Your workbook is never uploaded wholesale; only the specific cell values Rogo reads for your prompt are transmitted. It stores a workbook ID and file name (so you can resume sessions), plus conversation history.
Retention
	•	Conversation history is stored on Rogo’s servers, associated with your account (your messages, and in the plugin any cell data read during a session), so you can revisit past work.
	•	You can delete chats via the three-dots menu next to a chat name → “Delete Chat.” Note bulk history deletion isn’t currently available.
	•	Firms can have organization-level file retention policies, which your admin configures. The exact retention periods and whether/how your firm has set them are not something I can see or confirm — that’s tenant- and contract-specific.
Where it’s processed
The platform runs as a multi-model orchestration layer over frontier models from Anthropic, OpenAI, and Google (all US-based labs). The specific data-center regions/geography where processing occurs is not detailed in the documentation I can access, so I won’t assert a location.
For anything diligence-grade, go to the source
For formal answers on retention windows, data residency/processing location, sub-processors, encryption, and certifications (the kind of detail a security review or compliance team needs), use:
	•	trust.rogo.ai — Rogo’s trust and security policies
	•	Your Rogo account team / engagement manager, or support@rogo.ai
I’d treat the above as an accurate summary of Rogo’s stated practices, but confirm specifics with those sources before relying on them for a compliance sign-off.
