===== Slide 8 =====
WHY A PLATFORM, NOT AN APP  ·  THE MOAT
A compliant workflow platform is
the moat.
Standalone apps can’t win
Point tools are thin wrappers on someone else’s model. The model will eventually swallow that work, and banks and regulators won’t trust a wrapper with a live deal.
Compliance is the perimeter
Supervision, permissions, audit, and information barriers must live in the platform. Apps comply with it, or they don’t ship.
Own the workflows, not the model
The governed workflow graph, permissioned process data, and institutional memory are exactly what a wrapper can never replicate. Models are swappable; the layer a bank trusts is not.
