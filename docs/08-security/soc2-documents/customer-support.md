# Customer Support Channels

Conductia, Inc. Prepared 2026-10-25; reviewed and approved by Martin Casado.

Customers reach Conductia at **info@conductia.com**, the public inbox listed on the privacy, terms of use and accessibility pages of conductia.com. Privacy and data subject requests go to **privacy@conductia.com**, linked from the app's privacy page.

| Channel | Where published | Handling |
|----|----|----|
| info@conductia.com | conductia.com/privacy, /terms-of-use, /accessibility | Monitored by Dan; answered within 24 hours where a reply is needed |
| Contact form | conductia.com/contact (Netlify Forms with spam honeypot) | Reviewed by the same team; the page states replies come from a conductia.com address, usually within two business days |
| privacy@conductia.com | App privacy page | Privacy and data subject requests |

Customers are told about info@conductia.com during onboarding, which the team runs directly with each customer.
