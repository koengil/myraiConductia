## 2. Advisory board — former FINRA examiners

Former FINRA examiners are engaged as advisors. FINRA is the primary US regulator for
broker-dealers and investment banking communications. Having former examiners — the people
who ran the examinations other firms failed — on the advisory board is a direct signal of
compliance credibility. It also provides domain intelligence for building the compliance
layer: the advisors know what regulators look for and where institutional failures occur.

FINRA compliance consultants are also being engaged for the advisory board.
