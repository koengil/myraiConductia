## 1. The market

Investment banking is a market the source characterizes as "hundreds of billions" in
combined revenue (Goldman Sachs, JPMorgan scale). The workflows that generate that
revenue — M&A advisory, deal sourcing, pitch preparation, client communications — remain
heavily manual and structurally exposed to regulatory risk.

Source: `99-source/01-founder-call-storm.md` § Investment Banking AI Platform Vision

## 2. The immediate pain: compliance exposure

The most acute and commercially validated pain in investment banking is not a lack of
data or analysis capability. It is non-compliant communications. Investment banks face
multi-billion-dollar regulatory fines when communications breach FINRA and equivalent
standards. Senior bankers — managing directors, not junior analysts — are where most
compliance failures occur and where reputational damage from leaked or inappropriate
communications is most severe.

The cost justification is direct: non-compliant communications have drawn
multi-billion-dollar fines, and leaked inappropriate messages cause reputational damage
independent of the fine.

Source: `99-source/01-founder-call-storm.md` § Compliance Layer as Core Product
("multi-billion dollar fines for non-compliant communications"; "Reputational damage

## 3. The wedge: M&A, sell-side, compliance-first

Conductia does not attempt to be a general finance AI tool. The entry point is M&A
advisory for managing directors on the sell side. This is a specific, high-stakes workflow
where:

- The decision-maker is a senior principal, not a junior analyst
- Every communication is regulated
- The value of surfacing the right decision at the right moment is directly tied to deal
  outcomes worth tens to hundreds of millions

Starting with M&A sell-side creates a clearly scoped demo scenario (Atlassian, sell-side,
exploring acquirers), a credible first customer profile (MDs at investment banks), and a
compliance infrastructure requirement that is immediately defensible and differentiating.

Source: `99-source/02-team-build-notes.md` § Investment Banking AI Platform Vision;
`99-source/03-full-transcript-2026-05-19.md` (Lance articulating the focus on sell-side
M&A, managing directors, decisions not data)

The wedge is the entry point, not the ceiling. The long-term opportunity is a fully
automated investment bank — every step from initial client intent through research,
compliance, counterparty identification, and pitch preparation handled by the platform.
