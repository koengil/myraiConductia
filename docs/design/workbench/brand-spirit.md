# Conductia — Brand Spirit
Version: v13 · 2026-08-27 · companion to "v13 - Logo Directions.dc.html" and the Workbench Kit (v11/v12)
Sources: the product brief and repo docs read during extraction (docs/00-vision, 04-brand, decision log ADR-0002…0013), the shipped frontend, and the brand-language work (v05).

## 1. What Conductia is
A compliance-first M&A intelligence platform: the system investment banks run entire mandates on. Agentic execution (Casey) does the work between decisions; the banker's judgment is the product; compliance is the architecture, not a feature. Every material claim is sourced, every consequential action is screened before it happens, and every decision is reconstructable afterward.

## 2. Vision and mission
- Vision: every deal runs on rails — governed end to end, from conflict check to close, with nothing material done off the record.
- Mission: give Managing Directors an execution system they can hand to a regulator as readily as to a client — faster because it is governed, not despite it.
- The bet: in regulated finance, trust is the growth loop. The firms that win with AI will be the ones who can PROVE what it did.

## 3. Who it is for
Managing Directors and their deal teams — people who live in Bloomberg, FactSet, and Excel, are unimpressed by decoration, and are personally accountable for every external word. Secondary parties enter on strict boundaries: sellers (their room only), buyers (phase-gated, NDA), counsel, and the CCO (the supervisory queue).

## 4. The metaphor system (use it everywhere, never explain it twice)
- The Conductor: the MD. Tempo and judgment. The system never performs without them.
- Rails: the governed path a deal runs on — 5 phases, stations in sequence.
- Stations: units of work with a contract (inputs → output → gate). Done, skipped (recorded), or held — never silently passed.
- Casey: the AI actor. Drafts, screens, scores; never commits. Violet is Casey's color and appears nowhere else.
- Receipts: what every run leaves behind. The register is append-only and is the brand's proof of honesty.
- The Perimeter: who can see what. Code names outside the team. Boundaries are visible, not implied.

## 5. Design principles (the Workbench spirit, v11/v12)
1. Quiet authority. Warm neutrals, ink black as the only strong accent, hairlines over shadows. Nothing shouts; verdicts still land instantly.
2. Evidence over decoration. If an element doesn't inform a decision, it goes. Numbers are tabular, sourced, and dated.
3. Solid pastel verdicts, dot-category calm. Judgment chips are filled; classification is a quiet dot. Color = meaning, never mood.
4. One serif moment. Georgia appears only for the deal's identity and source excerpts — the letterhead inside the machine.
5. The human gate is sacred. Every AI output ends at a person. Primary buttons name the commit ("Approve 6 sends"), never "Continue".
6. Receipts are the voice. Confirmations state what was recorded, not how the product feels about it.
7. Restraint over flourish; motion is causality (100–160ms), never theater.

## 6. Voice
Senior, precise, decisive. Statements, not questions. Sentence case everywhere. No em-dashes in user-facing copy (regulatory separators excepted: the compliance status label "Blocked — not compliant" is a frozen regulatory status descriptor per ADR-0003 consequences and D15, and keeps its separator). Never: "magic", "supercharge", confidence percentages, exclamation marks. Always: name the actor, the action, the record ("Released by you. The override and rationale are in the register.").

## 7. Logo principles (governing the v13 directions)
- Single-color ink #191919; reversed white on ink; no gradients, no bevels, no motion.
- Pure geometry from the metaphor system: arcs (conduit/rails), dots (stations/receipts), ruled lines (the register), the circle (perimeter), the baton (the conductor).
- Must survive 16px (favicon), a document stamp, and embossing on paper.
- Violet may appear in at most one element and only as Casey (direction L6).
- Wordmark: Inter 650, -0.02em; serif alternate (Georgia) for letterhead contexts.
- Directions on the canvas: L1 Conduit (continuity) · L2 Seal (perimeter block) · L3 Rails · L4 Baton (the human) · L5 Register (receipts) · L6 Conduit-Signal (Casey dot) · L7 Station (maximum restraint) · L8 Letterhead (type as mark). Finalists to be selected by the owner; final lockups, clearspace, and exports follow selection.
