Three layers

Storm 07-08: "always-on is different than horizontal" — correct. The platform is three layers, not two. Click any app for detail.

Layer 1 — Platform (always-on substrate)

Runs regardless of any deal or step: security/isolation, audit log, access control, storage, search, comms surveillance, cost/time metering, knowledge graph. Built by Futureproofing (OS).

Layer 2 — Horizontal workflow rails

The engine that carries a deal through the 65 steps: orchestration, the next-step recommender (after each step, a ranked suggestion + full-step dropdown), QC gates, verification. Built by Futureproofing (OS). Layer 3 is the vertical step-skills on the rail above (RTS).
