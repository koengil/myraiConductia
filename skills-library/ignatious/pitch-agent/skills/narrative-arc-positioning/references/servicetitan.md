# ServiceTitan — IPO Roadshow, Dec 2024 (61 slides)

## 1. Company one-liner
"The Operating System for the Trades" — field-service SaaS platform for residential and commercial trades, covering job management, dispatch, payments, CRM, payroll, and customer engagement.

## 2. Narrative arc
- pp. 1–4: **Opening + mission** — title + disclaimer; **"Born in the Trades, Built for the Trades"** with founder/team imagery — mission-driven authenticity.
- pp. 5–8: **Investment highlights + market** — five-point framework: Massive Durable Market / Platform Leadership / Expanding Moat / Multiple Growth Vectors / Efficient Operating Model. $650B trades industry, $1.3B TAM upside vs. $685M SAM.
- pp. 8–10: **Platform position** — "Operating System" across six workflow pillars (Lead Gen → Book Job → Dispatch → Perform Job → Payment & Invoice → Back Office Ops); customer case studies (30% CAGR to 6x revenue growth).
- pp. 10–11: **Product roadmap + moat** — 2021–2024+ expansion across CRM, Field Pro, Field Pro+, Payments Pro; replication-difficulty narrative.
- pp. 11–13: **Market penetration** — three-phase strategy: deepen existing trades → expand to new trades → addressable trades + markets; long runway.
- pp. 12–14: **Financial foundations** — Q2 FY25 snapshot: $685M revenue, 24% YoY growth, 23% operating growth; **"Now is the Time"** closing message.
- pp. 14–15: **Closing thesis** — massive opportunity + decades-long runway + becoming industry standard + changing customer lives + team conviction.
- pp. 16–21: **Appendix opening** — trades supporting society imagery; $1.5T trades spend; $6M+ trade businesses; >75% non-discretionary spend.
- pp. 30–40: **Operating leverage** — 77% gross margin; 110% NDR; 21-month CAC payback; revenue trajectory ($101M → $193M Q1'23 → Q2'25); 23% GTV YoY; non-GAAP margin to 7%; 60-day sales cycle; 95% GDR.
- p. 43: **Team** — purpose-built, exceptional team; best-in-class hires; trades-industry experts.
- pp. 44–61: **Detailed appendix** — definitions, reconciliations, financial tables.

## 3. Positioning moves
- **Vertical-SaaS category leadership** — "The Operating System" for field services is platform-of-record language, not point-solution.
- **Trades-focused mission authenticity** — "Born in the Trades, Built for the Trades" frames founder/team credibility as organizational DNA, not marketing overlay.
- **Platform-of-record for an underserved industry** — $650B+ market + 75% non-discretionary spend + 6M+ fragmented businesses = massive TAM with limited incumbent competition.
- **Multi-product expansion narrative** — roadmap from core FSM/HCM/ERP/CRM to adjacent Payments Pro / FinTech / Field Pro+ demonstrates moat expansion + expansion-revenue opportunity.
- **Operating leverage story** — 110% NDR + 77% GM + 21-month CAC payback + path to 7% non-GAAP op margin = scalable, efficient model.
- **Land-expand-upgrade** — customers start with core, expand through subscription add-ons and FinTech; 95%+ platform revenue.

## 4. Opening hook (pp. 1–4)
Clean title + disclaimer + offering summary, then page 4: **"BORN IN THE TRADES, BUILT FOR THE TRADES"** in bold white text with founding-team photos. Blue-collar worker imagery throughout. This is the foundational truth slide — authentic mission framing delivered before any product claim. **Hook works because it's not a slogan — it's a credentialing move.**

## 5. Closing move (pp. 14–15 + p. 43)
The narrative close is page 14's **"Now is the Time"** layering five reinforcing messages: massive opportunity, early innings of decades-long runway, becoming industry standard, changing customer lives, passionate team. Then page 43's "We Have the Team to Execute." Then pure appendix (definitions, reconciliations, financial tables). The "early innings of a decades-long growth runway" line alone is the closing thesis — justifies long-duration investment on both TAM expansion *and* product expansion axes.

## 7. What's distinctive
ServiceTitan positions a vertical-SaaS platform by **making authenticity load-bearing** — "born in the trades" is organizational DNA, not a marketing overlay. The narrative moves from mission-driven opening → massive market opportunity (real spend data) → platform stickiness via customer case studies (2x–6x revenue growth) → operating-leverage proof (110% NDR, 21-month CAC payback). Rather than competing on features, ServiceTitan positions itself as the operating system integrating workflows across six mission-critical functions, creating a widening moat through product expansion competitors can't easily replicate. The **"early innings of decades-long growth"** frame signals both TAM expansion (new trades, new geographies) and product expansion (FSM → platform) — a long-duration thesis for institutional investors.
