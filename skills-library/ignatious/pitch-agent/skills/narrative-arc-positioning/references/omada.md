# Omada Health — IPO Roadshow, June 2025 (21 slides)

## 1. Company one-liner
Omada is a digital health platform delivering virtual care *between* doctor's visits — a "between-visit provider" for chronic conditions (hypertension, diabetes, obesity, COPD, GLP-1 care).

## 2. Narrative arc
- pp. 1–3: **Opening hook** — "Building a Between-Visit Provider" / "Bend the curve" — establishes the category and mission.
- pp. 4–5: **Problem** — 80%+ members miss engagement opportunities between visits; chronic-condition epidemic; between-visit gaps as the specific pain.
- pp. 6–7: **Product positioning** — "Compassionate Intelligence" (the human layer) meets technology; purpose-built care team platform.
- pp. 8–10: **Clinical evidence** — 55%+ 12-mo persistence, 50%+ 24-mo; 5.4M clinical data points; multi-condition outcomes.
- p. 10: **Competitive four-quadrant** — Evidence, Compassion, Technology, Operational excellence — positions Omada as set apart.
- p. 11: **Go-to-market** — diverse customer-centric model reaching employers, health plans, health systems.
- p. 12: **PBM expansion** — 8M+ eligible members via one of the largest PBMs.
- p. 13: **Competitive positioning** — market share chart; IPO framed as "a moment to seize."
- p. 14: **GLP-1 growth vector** — integrated tracks; expanded cardiometabolic demand.
- p. 15: **Enhanced outcomes** — +28% weight loss from GLP-1 care track.
- pp. 16–18: **Financial performance** — 33% members CAGR, 30% revenue CAGR; NDR; 2.7x / 3.9x quarterly revenue + non-GAAP gross profit expansion.
- p. 19: **Team** — healthcare + tech backgrounds.
- p. 20: **Summary** — differentiation recap + "Thank you."
- p. 21: **Appendix** — 2026 model considerations + GAAP reconciliation.

## 3. Positioning moves
- **Between-visit category framing** — Omada is not device/app/telehealth replacement; it's a discrete care provider in the underserved window where disease progression actually happens.
- **Payer/employer/PBM channel diversification** — reaches employers + health plans + health systems + one of the largest PBMs (8M+ members).
- **Outcomes-based ROI** — value articulated via a 4-quadrant (evidence, compassion, technology, ops) and NDR that proves pricing power.
- **"Compassionate Intelligence"** — technology + human empathy; seamless member journey designed around individuals.
- **"Bend the curve"** — engagement during the critical between-visit period where 80%+ miss opportunities.
- **Multi-condition expansion** — land in cardiometabolic (HTN, diabetes, obesity, post-GLP-1), expand to COPD and adjacent conditions.

## 4. Opening hook (pp. 1–3)
Human-centered imagery (person in orange chair) + "Building a Between-Visit Provider" + "Bend the curve." Page 3 pivots to member-experience smartphone UI with the mission: "deliver unvalidated virtual care between doctor's visits through a simple, elegant, and seamless experience for both members and buyers." **Frames Omada as solving a specific, named problem** (the between-visit gap) rather than a general health-tech play.

## 5. Closing move (pp. 20–21)
"Omada in Summary" recaps the differentiators (outcomes-based, transparent pricing, diverse customer mix, scalable ops). A hero image + "Thank you." Appendix — 2026 model considerations + reconciliation. Closes on **evidence of execution, not future potential.**

## 7. What's distinctive
Omada reframes digital health **not as a consumer app or telehealth substitute** but as a clinical care provider operating in the between-visit window where most chronic-disease progression occurs. Unlike competitors focused on continuous monitoring or on-demand access, Omada positions itself as a **discrete care provider with compassionate intelligence** — human judgment augmented by data — selling on economic impact (NDR, multi-condition revenue) and a narrowly defined value proposition. Multi-condition expansion + GLP-1 play signals land-and-expand grounded in outcomes, not feature novelty.
