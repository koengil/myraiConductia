# OneStream — IPO Roadshow, July 2024 (50 slides)

## 1. Company one-liner
"The operating system for modern finance" — a unified cloud platform replacing fragmented legacy ERP/EPM systems and point solutions with a single AI-powered solution for financial planning, close, and reporting.

## 2. Narrative arc
- pp. 1–4: **Opening** — company overview + offering + vision: "To be the operating system for modern finance."
- pp. 8–10: **Problem (Why Now)** — four macro drivers (competitive environment, legacy end-of-life, finance talent shortage, data complexity); CFO fragmented across dozens of functions.
- pp. 9–11: **Organizational complexity** — CFO at center of 30+ interconnected systems; fragmented financial processes.
- pp. 12–15: **Solution** — OneStream as unified platform; single source of truth; extensible architecture with 90+ partner applications.
- p. 16: **AI differentiation** — proprietary AI (Generative AI, StandardAI, Scenario Clarity, Curate, RaaS) built on transparency, auditability, repeatability.
- pp. 17–18: **Growth thesis** — four pillars (amplify the core, expand finance-led operations, extend enterprise finance AI, increase platform power).
- pp. 19–20: **GTM + traction** — land-and-expand; 1,423 customers across manufacturing, consumer, FS, healthcare, tech, retail, telecom, government.
- pp. 21–25: **Customer cases** — Autoliv, Ingram Micro, Stake Center; 28–36% ACV uplift; 3–4.4x expansion.
- pp. 27–29: **Market + growth levers** — $850B Finance + Operations TAM; $10B legacy replacement; six long-term levers.
- pp. 30–31: **Values + team** — Innovation, Respect, Accountability; founder-led leadership.
- pp. 31–42: **Financial model** — hybrid pricing (seat + usage + platform); ARR growth 34–67% YoY; 69% gross margin; 20% long-term operating target.
- pp. 43–50: **Conclusion + appendix** — vision restatement bookend; GAAP reconciliations, definitions.

## 3. Positioning moves
- **Office-of-the-CFO platform** — consolidates planning, close, reporting, analysis, forecasting into a single extensible platform rather than orchestration across 30+ systems.
- **Legacy EPM disruption** — directly contrasts with Oracle Hyperion, SAP, NetSuite; frames legacy EPM as end-of-life.
- **Unified platform vs. point solutions** — core finance + financial applications + operations + AI through single architecture.
- **Applied AI as core (not bolted on)** — proprietary AI strategy with transparency/auditability/repeatability as design principles.
- **Extensibility + ecosystem** — 90+ partner integrations via Solution Exchange = "app store" model for an operating system.
- **Subscription-model predictability** — 88–96% subscription revenue with strong retention vs. legacy perpetual-license model.
- **Land-and-expand (vertical + geographic)** — sales teams aligned by vertical + geography; land on core, expand to apps, ops, AI.

## 4. Opening hook (pp. 1–5)
Vision slide in large white text on aspirational imagery: **"To be the operating system for modern finance."** The term "operating system" invokes both ubiquity (Windows/macOS) and mission-critical status. A concentric-circle diagram (core finance → financial apps → operational apps → applied AI) encapsulates the entire positioning visually. Compact, memorable, and immediately structural.

## 5. Closing move (pp. 43–50)
Page 43 reiterates the vision statement — **bookending the deck with "operating system for modern finance."** Then appendix: supplementary materials, definitions, GAAP reconciliations. The repetition of the vision slide reinforces the singular strategic message more than an emotional uplift would.

## 6. Signature phrases
- "The operating system for modern finance"
- "Single source of truth"
- "Unified, cloud-based platform"
- "Modernizing and unifying core financial functions"
- "Empowering the CFO to become a critical driver of business strategy"

## 7. What's distinctive
OneStream positions itself **not as a point solution or best-in-class suite, but as an operating system** — a deliberate architectural claim implying both ubiquity and indispensability. Three interlocking advantages: (1) truly unified cloud-native platform consolidating 30+ legacy systems; (2) applied AI built into core product logic (not grafted on), with transparency/auditability as design principles; (3) extensibility via 90+ partner integrations and developer community — the "app store" model for the Office of the CFO. The vision-statement bookend (page 4 → page 43) shows uncommon discipline in keeping the single metaphor load-bearing across 50 slides.
