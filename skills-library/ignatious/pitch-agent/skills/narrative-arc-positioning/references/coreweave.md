# CoreWeave — IPO Roadshow, March 2025 (20 slides)

## 1. Company one-liner
"The AI Hyperscaler" — the world's leading GPU cloud infrastructure provider purpose-built for AI compute workloads.

## 2. Narrative arc
- pp. 1–2: **Opener** — "Powering AI Innovation" + "The AI Hyperscaler" category claim.
- p. 3: **Market frame** — GPU workloads fundamentally different from general compute (architecture, topology, orchestration).
- pp. 4–5: **Technology architecture** — purpose-built platform; pending Weights & Biases acquisition; performance + efficiency.
- p. 6: **Execution credentials** — 250K GPUs, 360MW, first-to-market with GB200; OpenAI $11.9B strategic deal as validation.
- p. 7: **Geographic footprint** — distributed North America + Europe.
- p. 8: **Customer pyramid** — tiered base anchored by hyperscaler-grade names.
- p. 9: **Land-and-expand** — 15x growth within accounts over time.
- p. 10: **Team execution** — ability to manage hypergrowth + infrastructure scale.
- pp. 11–13: **Scale + growth narrative** — $1.29B revenue, 73% growth, 17% EBITDA margin; RPO and take-or-pay contracts; "CoreWeave Model" explains margin expansion mechanics.
- p. 14: **Unit economics** — revenue drivers and asset base growth.
- p. 15: **Scaling model** — sustained revenue acceleration, EBITDA expansion.
- p. 16: **Capital structure** — designed for rapid scaling at lower cost.
- pp. 17–20: **Appendix** — financial reconciliations.

## 3. Positioning moves
- **GPU-native category creation** — explicit rejection of general-purpose cloud; AI compute is a distinct infrastructure class.
- **Hyperscaler-alternative framing** — "The AI Hyperscaler": a focused specialist vs. conglomerate cloud providers splitting attention across services.
- **First-mover in bleeding-edge accelerators** — NVIDIA GB200 first-to-market; OpenAI $11.9B deal as category validation.
- **NVIDIA partnership as lever** — joint positioning on latest hardware; direct relationship enables rapid deployment of next-gen chips.
- **Infrastructure as moat** — 250K+ GPUs, geographic distribution, continuous capex framed as defensible advantage.
- **Contract economics as durability** — 4-year weighted-average contracts, 15–25% prepayment, 96% committed, 2.5-year payback.

## 4. Opening hook (pp. 1–3)
Pages 1–2 lead with "Powering AI Innovation" and the category definition "The AI Hyperscaler." Page 3 builds the moat by explaining why parallel AI workloads require fundamentally different architecture than traditional cloud. The hook puts investors on the thesis that GPU cloud is a distinct category where one company can own the hyperscaler position.

## 5. Closing move (pp. 15–16)
Operations model + long-term targets, then capital structure designed for rapid scaling. Closes on contract-economics durability: "we have the unit economics, the playbook, and the capital structure to execute at scale."

## 6. Signature phrases
- "The AI Hyperscaler"
- "Purpose-built for AI"
- "Bleeding-Edge Compute"
- "CoreWeave Model"
- "First-to-Market Track Record"

## 7. What's distinctive
Pure category-creation pitch. By framing GPU cloud as a distinct category that requires different architecture than general-purpose cloud, CoreWeave positions itself not as a cloud alternative but as a new infrastructure class. "AI Hyperscaler" elevates the company above the startup tier while maintaining credibility against the hyperscalers, leveraging proof points (OpenAI deal, GB200 first-to-market) and operational execution (scale, capex, contract economics) to argue that focused specialization beats conglomerate optionality in an AI-native era.
