# Hinge Health — IPO Roadshow, May 2025 (26 slides)

## 1. Company one-liner
AI-powered digital MSK (musculoskeletal) care platform that enables employers and payers to deliver accessible, personalized pain relief and recovery at scale.

## 2. Narrative arc
- pp. 1–2: **Vision + offering** — "build a new health system that transforms outcomes, experience and automate the delivery of care."
- pp. 3–4: **Problem** — MSK is ~40% of total medical spend, "largely ignored"; 9% of members drive the majority of cost; "inverse care law" (highest need, lowest access).
- pp. 4–5: **Platform positioning** — "Reimagined MSK care"; AI-guided care teams; TrueMoRion integrated AI; multi-modal spectrum of care.
- pp. 6–7: **Product foundation** — proprietary AI-driven database for personalization; AI-augmented care team acceleration.
- pp. 8–9: **Outcomes validation** — ~95% engagement lift; clinical-grade outcomes across diverse demographics.
- pp. 10–11: **Market penetration** — 50+ employers; 5 of 5 national plans; 3 of 3 regional plans; 100% payer satisfaction.
- pp. 12–14: **Competitive differentiation** — four-quadrant positioning (scale/speed/outcomes/integration); peer-reviewed 68% pain reduction, 67% engagement; direct ROI for payers/employers.
- pp. 15–20: **GTM + financials** — targeted sales model; partnership distribution; ~35% non-GAAP margins; operating leverage.
- p. 21: **Investment summary** — $340B+ market; integrated partnerships; proven outcomes; efficient SaaS model.
- pp. 22–26: **Appendix + thank you.**

## 3. Positioning moves
- **MSK-category framing** — reframes musculoskeletal care as a hidden 40% of medical spend, not an orthopedic vertical.
- **Payer/employer ROI** — direct financial savings proof (50+ employers, all 5 national plans, 100% satisfaction).
- **Outcomes-based validation** — peer-reviewed clinical efficacy (68% pain reduction, 67% engagement) — rigor beats tech-enthusiasm.
- **Human-in-loop AI** — "AI-augmented care team" reduces friction, doesn't replace clinicians.
- **Clinical-grade digital** — anchors every capability claim to either clinical data or customer integration depth.
- **Distribution-as-moat** — 100% national/regional payer penetration converts traction into category leadership evidence.

## 4. Opening hook (pp. 1–3)
Vision statement first, then the hidden-cost crisis: MSK as ~40% of medical spend "largely ignored" in healthcare reform, with 9% of members driving majority spend. The "inverse care law" frames the mission. This hooks investors on a big, quantified, underserved problem before any product claim.

## 5. Closing move (p. 21)
Investment highlights anchored in hard metrics (margin expansion, member growth), payer/employer momentum (100% satisfaction, full national coverage), and clinical validation (peer-reviewed studies). Leaves the investor with proof of market fit and financial-model durability — not with a vision promise.

## 6. Signature phrases
- "Build a new health system that transforms outcomes, experience and automate the delivery of care"
- "Accessible and personalized MSK care"
- "Proprietary AI-driven database for personalization, engagement at scale"
- "Prove clinical and financial outcomes for clients"
- "Efficient operating model with strong margin profile"

## 7. What's distinctive
Hinge positions MSK **not as an orthopedic vertical but as a hidden-cost, high-volume category** ripe for digital-first disruption via integrated payer/employer partnerships. Unlike point solutions (telemedicine, PT apps), Hinge frames itself as a *system* — guided care teams + proprietary AI + spectrum-of-care routing + evidence-backed outcomes + payer distribution. Distinct discipline: every capability claim is anchored to clinical data or customer integration depth. Distribution wins become evidence of category leadership, not just traction. Investable as a high-volume, low-cost-of-care play — not as digital health novelty.
