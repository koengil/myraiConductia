# SailPoint — IPO Roadshow, Feb 2025 (26 slides) — re-IPO post Thoma Bravo take-private

## 1. Company one-liner
The intelligent identity security platform managing access to applications and data across human *and* non-human identities in the enterprise.

## 2. Narrative arc
- pp. 1–3: **Opening** — "Defining the next generation of identity security"; **re-introduction framework** explicitly contrasting "what is the same" (mission, culture, market position) with "what is different" (identity expansion, evolution to platform, software-to-SaaS, AI-enabled portfolio).
- pp. 4–5: **Market** — $824M ARR baseline; $555B expanding market driven by explosion of identity types and SaaS.
- pp. 6–7: **Problem** — risk and complexity from data proliferation and non-human identity emergence; identity as foundation of enterprise security.
- pp. 8–9: **Solution architecture** — IGA + Privileged Task Automation evolving into "comprehensive, extensible, identity-first security platform."
- pp. 10–11: **Competitive positioning** — "why we win" on breadth, velocity, features; 2,895 global customers, 60%+ Fortune 500.
- pp. 11–12: **GTM + ecosystem** — land / cross-sell / up-sell pyramid + 80+ partners.
- p. 13: **Five growth levers** — new logos; expansion; portfolio leverage; global; portfolio expansion.
- pp. 14–16: **SaaS transformation** — 55% → 62% SaaS ARR mix; 60% of new SaaS ARR from new logos; total ARR growth 30%; SaaS growth 40%.
- pp. 17–18: **Retention + margin** — DBNR 104–105%; adjusted gross profit 73–81%; subscription gross profit 84–86%.
- p. 19: **Rule of 40** — 30% ARR growth + 14% adj. op margin = 44.
- pp. 20–21: **Culture + team + takeaways** — founder-led leadership; seven key takeaways.
- pp. 22–26: **Appendix + "Thank you!"**

## 3. Positioning moves
- **Identity as foundational security paradigm** — from siloed governance to identity as cornerstone in an era of data explosion + non-human identity proliferation.
- **Non-human identity expansion** — machine identity security + privileged task automation as core capabilities responding to IoT/API/microservice sprawl.
- **SaaS-first modernization** — 62% SaaS ARR mix + 40% SaaS growth signals transformation beyond legacy software.
- **Re-IPO as strategic reset** — "(Re)introducing SailPoint" avoids take-private stigma; explicitly separates what endured from what transformed.
- **AI-era identity framing** — comprehensive platform + AI-enabled portfolio as native response to AI/data explosion.
- **Rule of 40 status** — 30% ARR growth + 14% adj. op margin as signal of maturity for public markets.

## 4. Opening hook (pp. 1–3)
Title: "Defining the next generation of identity security." The re-introduction move is the hook: a two-column **"What is the same / What is different"** framework. Same: mission, culture, market strength, product innovation. Different: data/identity/app-type expansion, governance→platform evolution, software→SaaS transition, AI-enabled portfolio. The framework is **permission-granting**: "we're the same stable company, but transformed."

## 5. Closing move (pp. 20–26)
Leadership team + seven numbered key takeaways (execution, innovation, partnership, culture, founder-led stability). Appendix: metrics/guidance, pro forma cap, cloud suite tiers, financial summary, definitions, GAAP reconciliations. Closes "Thank you!" — **simple and confident**, not defensive.

## 6. Signature phrases

- "SailPoint is strategically positioned to win the new identity security paradigm"

## 7. What's distinctive
The re-IPO context is **neither leaned into heavily nor glossed over — it's reframed as a strategic reset and expansion narrative.** The "(Re)introducing SailPoint" framing directly acknowledges history but positions it as reinvention, not recovery. The two-column "same/different" matrix grants investors permission to view the private period as a development phase, not a detour: mission + market leadership preserved while identity attack surface (non-human identity), GTM model (SaaS), and AI capabilities expanded. Avoids both boastfulness ("we're back!") and defensiveness. Implicit story: Thoma Bravo's tenure was productive technical + commercial work now being taken public at an inflection point. **Template for any company going public a second time.**
