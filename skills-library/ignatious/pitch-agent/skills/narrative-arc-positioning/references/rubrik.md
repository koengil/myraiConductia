# Rubrik — IPO Roadshow, April 2024 (62 slides)

## 1. Company one-liner
The world's leading cyber-resilience platform, securing data across every enterprise environment through automated recovery and Zero Trust architecture.

## 2. Narrative arc
- pp. 1–6: **Mission + urgency** — "Securing the World's Data"; data, regulations, and attacks exploding simultaneously.
- pp. 7–9: **Problem reframe** — "Prevention Alone is Not Enough"; shift from prevention-only cybersecurity to cyber resilience.
- pp. 10–17: **Platform architecture** — Rubrik Security Cloud with three pillars (Data Resilience, Data Observability, Data Remediation); holistic cyber resilience across the attack surface.
- pp. 18–26: **Market leadership** — recognized leadership in cyber-recovery; $36B market opportunity; 67K+ target enterprises; flywheel-driven GTM.
- pp. 22–26: **GenAI catalyst** — Rubrik as the trust layer for enterprise AI adoption.
- pp. 27–30: **Customer proof** — world-leading enterprises; positioning across prevention, resilience, and data categories.
- pp. 31–33: **Management + growth history** — exec team; rapid subscription ARR growth.
- pp. 34–50: **Financial performance** — high-growth subscription business; Cloud ARR trajectory; land-expand-extend motion; unit economics.
- pp. 55–56: **Closing strategic position** — AI × Data × Security convergence; Rubrik at the intersection.
- pp. 57–62: **Appendix** — GAAP reconciliations, FCF path, definitions.

## 3. Positioning moves
- **Cyber resilience category reframe** — shifts cybersecurity paradigm from prevention-centric to resilience-centric; "cyber resilience is the future."
- **Ransomware-era framing** — exploding data, regulations, and cyberattacks as proof of prevention failure and necessity of recovery-first.
- **Zero Trust Data Security** — foundational architecture principle across all products; modern security posture as essential.
- **Platform consolidation** — Security Cloud as unified replacement for point solutions across defense, observability, remediation.
- **Microsoft partnership lever** — strategic integrations with Azure, Defender, etc. as distribution + validation mechanism.
- **GenAI as growth catalyst** — Rubrik as "necessary trust layer" for enterprises adopting generative AI safely.

## 4. Opening hook (pp. 1–3)
"Our Mission: Securing the World's Data" immediately asserts scope. Offering and scale pages establish $784M ARR with 47% growth, $53B market opportunity by 2027, and 6,100+ customers. Hook = **scale achieved + opportunity massively larger** — urgency without hype.

## 5. Closing move (pp. 55–56)
"Our Strategic Position Offers Significant Opportunity" — a visual convergence of **AI + Data + Security intersecting at Rubrik.** Appendix confirms GAAP reconciliations and FCF path to breakeven, ending on strong unit economics as a scaled, profitable-trajectory security platform.

## 6. Signature phrases
- "Securing the World's Data"
- "Prevention Alone is Not Enough"
- "Cyber Resilience is Required"
- "Holistic Cyber Resilience"
- "Rubrik provides the necessary trust layer for enterprises to adopt GenAI"
- "Data Security is the Future of Cybersecurity"

## 7. What's distinctive
Rubrik **pivots away from endpoint/network prevention** (CrowdStrike, Palo Alto, Zscaler) into **data-centric recovery and observability as the core security strategy**. Rather than "how do we prevent breaches?", Rubrik answers "how do we ensure data survives and recovers from any attack?" Reinforced by architecture (time-series metadata, Zero Trust, cloud-native), market narrative (cyber resilience replaces prevention), and customer metrics (land-expand-extend across enterprise, cloud, SaaS, unstructured data). The tie to GenAI legitimizes new revenue streams (data trust, AI data governance) while converting a legacy *backup* narrative into a modern **cyber-resilience + data-governance** necessity.
