# Netskope — IPO Roadshow, Sep 2025 (39 slides)

## 1. Company one-liner
"Security and Networking ReAimagined" — a cloud-native security + networking platform that unifies SWG, firewall, data protection, and SASE capabilities to protect enterprises adopting AI and cloud.

## 2. Narrative arc
- pp. 1–3: **Setup + credibility** — "Security and Networking ReAimagined"; $707M ARR, 220+ customers, $149B TAM; "AI and Cloud have completely revolutionized work."
- pp. 4–6: **Problem** — legacy infrastructure cannot address the modern digital landscape; data-in-motion is unsolved; appliances fail against cloud-native threats.
- pp. 7–12: **Solution architecture** — converged security/networking/analytics platform; deep tech in API understanding, data security, edge processing; AI models across platform; "Netskope One Gateway" extends to on-prem edge.
- pp. 13–18: **Market position + wins** — category leadership across enterprise/SMB/mid-market; >30% of Fortune 500; global network, 99.999% uptime.
- pp. 19–20: **Growth engines** — $74B greenfield + legacy replacement; multiple avenues (SASE, SSL/TLS inspection, DLP, advanced threat detection).
- pp. 21–24: **Expansion + execution** — land-and-expand motion; 35% YoY ARR growth; financial highlights.
- pp. 25–28: **Unit economics** — NRR expansion; non-GAAP margin expansion; long-term GAAP target model.
- pp. 29–39: **Appendix** — GTM metrics, convertible notes, GAAP reconciliations, definitions.

## 3. Positioning moves
- **SASE category ownership** — Netskope as the unified platform replacing VPN, SSL inspection, firewall, and DLP in hybrid-work/cloud environments.
- **Zero Trust via API visibility** — "language of the modern internet" (APIs, SaaS, cloud) as foundational to Zero Trust architecture.
- **Data-centric security** — "Proactively Identify and Protect Sensitive Data" as the differentiator, not just network controls.
- **Cloud-native vs. legacy appliance** — Netskope's architecture is cloud-native, eliminating appliance sprawl and bottlenecks legacy firewalls can't overcome.
- **AI/ML embedded, not bolted on** — proprietary AI models infused throughout platform for threat detection, classification, and anomaly detection.
- **Enterprise credibility proof** — >30% of Fortune 500 and ~18% of Forbes Global 5000; deep verticals (telecom, FS, healthcare).

## 4. Opening hook (pp. 1–3)
Scale + impact combo: $707M ARR in 4 years, 220+ customers, $149B TAM, plus dominance metrics (89% phishing blocked, 75x reduction in lateral movement risk, 74% of data protected). Anchors investor confidence before any architectural claim. "ReAimagined" (with a capital A) explicitly signals a reset of the category, not an incremental improvement.

## 5. Closing move (p. 29 + appendix)
"Netskope Key Takeaways" condenses into six bullets (first-to-market + flexible platform; data protection with SSL visibility; flexible deployment; established platforms driving adoption; experienced management; operational leverage). Then pure financial rigor (reconciliations, quarterly results). Closes on **operational credibility and financial rigor, not emotional uplift** — reinforces the "scaled, profitable" frame.

## 7. What's distinctive
Netskope's narrative centers on the **convergence of three forces — cloud adoption, remote work, and API-first architecture** — that have made legacy security infrastructure (firewalls, VPNs, appliances) obsolete. Unlike point-solution competitors, Netskope positions its platform as the single control plane for visibility, protection, and enforcement across all internet traffic and data flows. The distinctive claim is not "Zero Trust" or "SASE" alone, but **deep technical ownership of the modern internet's language** (APIs, cloud services, data flows) combined with proven enterprise GTM execution. This positions Netskope as category leader AND technology leader on the unsolved problem of data protection in motion.
