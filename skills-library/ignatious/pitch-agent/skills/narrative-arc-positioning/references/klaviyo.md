# Klaviyo — IPO Roadshow, 2023 (40 slides)

## 1. Company one-liner
Klaviyo is a data intelligence + marketing automation platform that lets DTC brands own their customer data and coordinate personalized messaging across email, SMS, and push — turning owned data into owned growth.

## 2. Narrative arc
- pp. 1–3: **Opener** — offering summary; the visual thesis: "Own your data" → "Own your growth"; CEO video frames the core problem (unrefined batch messaging, cobbled workflows).
- pp. 2–5: **Solution framework** — aggregated data store + real-time action + segmentation + AI/ML = coordinated engagement.
- pp. 6–10: **Platform architecture** — layered stack (data store → segmentation, profiles, events → analytics → campaigns/messaging → apps).
- pp. 11–14: **Data moat** — 6.9B unified profiles; cohort journey visualization; guided consumer intelligence (predictive analytics, A/B testing, benchmarks).
- pp. 14–16: **Product simplicity** — automated flows, multi-channel coordination (email, SMS, push) with CDP integration.
- pp. 18–20: **Competitive positioning** — CDP + personalization "completes the DTC tech stack" (alongside Shopify, Wix); 300+ integrations.
- p. 21: **GTM motion** — efficient inbound (self-serve, word-of-mouth, partners) + targeted outbound (enterprise).
- pp. 22–31: **Market + growth** — $68B TAM; 130K+ customers; $5K ARPU; 1,453 customers >$50K ARR; 55%+ YoY growth at $585M LTM.
- pp. 31–35: **Unit economics** — 128% cohort expansion; 71% gross margins; OpEx leverage.
- pp. 35–38: **Path to profitability** — from negative op income to positive; FCF generation; long-term 20%+ non-GAAP operating margin target.
- pp. 37–40: **Closing financials** — long-term model + GAAP reconciliations.

## 3. Positioning moves
- **Owned-channels vs. paid-media** — the thesis: customer data ownership as moat; owned channels (email, SMS) vs. dependency on paid ad platforms.
- **Data as persistent moat** — 6.9B unified profiles + behavioral event tracking = network effects competitors cannot replicate.
- **DTC stack completion** — positions Klaviyo as the missing customer-data layer beside Shopify/Wix; not competitive with them, but the piece that completes the stack.
- **SMB-to-enterprise progression** — low-touch self-serve scales to enterprise (1,453 >$50K ARR customers, 94% YoY growth).
- **Platform > point-solution** — multi-channel infra, open APIs, 300+ integrations = extensible platform, not email vendor.
- **AI/ML-guided intelligence** — "guided consumer intelligence" reduces friction for SMBs without data science teams.

## 4. Opening hook (pp. 1–3)
The visual thesis is the hook: **"Own your data" → "Own your growth"** — two circles connected by five capabilities. Then the problem narrative via CEO video: marketers stuck sending unrefined batch blasts and guessing if they worked. The two-noun tagline converts a platform claim into a strategic posture every DTC operator intuitively understands.

## 5. Closing move (pp. 37–40)
Closes on **long-term operating model (20%+ non-GAAP operating margin target), FCF trajectory (25%+ long-term FCF margin), and GAAP reconciliations.** The narrative close is margin discipline — margins expand as the business matures, signaling a durable, high-leverage SaaS model.

## 6. Signature phrases
- "Own your data" / "Own your growth"
- "Coordinated engagement for frictionless cross-channel connectivity"
- "Guided consumer intelligence"
- "Completes the direct-to-consumer tech stack"
- "Efficient, rapid growth at scale"

## 7. What's distinctive
Klaviyo's pitch centers on **unified customer data ownership as the moat**, not multi-channel infrastructure alone. The deck explicitly argues that fragmented or platform-locked customer data (Shopify, Wix) creates strategic dependency; Klaviyo inverts this by making brands' own data the foundation. The narrative arc is textbook — problem (cobbled batch messaging) → tech (layered platform) → proof (6.9B profiles, 128% cohort expansion, 55%+ growth) → resilience (path to 20%+ margins). What makes it work is the discipline to keep the two-noun thesis ("own data, own growth") load-bearing across every downstream slide.
